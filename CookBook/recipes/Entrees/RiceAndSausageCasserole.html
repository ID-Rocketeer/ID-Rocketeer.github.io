Rice & Sausage Casserole
Ex Culina:Patricia Baldus

Ingredients:

1 pound pork sausage
1 green pepper
1 large onion
1 cup celery
1 cup water
1 can chicken rice soup
1 can cream of mushroom soup
1 cup uncooked rice (not instant)

Directions:

  1. Fry sausage, then onion, pepper and celery.
  2. Combine all ingredients in casserole.
  3. Bake 1½ hours at 300°.

Comments:My family did not like the pepper, celery, and onion pieces so I put all in the blender with a little bit of the water and liquified them and they liked that a lot better and it still had all those flavors.
