Gingersnaps
Ex Culina: Renelda Baldus

Ingredients:

¾ cup shortening
1 cup sugar
¼ cup dark molasses (Brer Rabbit or other)
1 egg
2 cups sifted flour
1 teaspoon cinnamon
½ teaspoon cloves
½ teaspoon ginger
½ teaspoon salt
2 teaspoons baking soda

Directions:

  1. Cream shortening and sugar together.
  2. Add molasses and egg. Beat well.
  3. Sift together flour, soda, salt and spices; add to first mixture. Mix well.
  4. Chill for ½ hour.
  5. Form one inch balls, roll in granulated sugar and place on greased cookie sheets 2 inches apart.
  6. Bake at 375° 8 - 10 minutes.

Notes:

  • Bake at 350° 8-10 min. - Makes a softer cookie.
  • "This is my grandma's recipe. Hope you like them. Brian does."
